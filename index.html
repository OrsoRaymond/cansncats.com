Who We Are

Tech conversations for people who want the truth—not the sales pitch.

Cans n Cats is an independent, listener-led podcast about AI, cybersecurity, privacy, and the technology changing daily life. We built the show for curious people who want straight answers without corporate jargon, fearmongering, or fake hype.

Listeners help choose what we investigate. We take the questions people are already asking and turn them into clear, honest conversations anyone can follow.
